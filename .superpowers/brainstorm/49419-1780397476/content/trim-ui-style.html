How should the trim UI appear?

When you want to trim a video in IINA, how should the trim controls show up?

A

Overlay panel on the video

A slim trim bar slides up over the bottom of the video — similar to how YouTube's trim editor works. Start/end handles on a timeline, trim while the video is playing or paused.

B

Side panel / drawer

A panel opens alongside the video (like IINA's playlist panel). Shows a waveform/thumbnail strip, start/end time inputs, and a Save button. Video keeps playing in the main area.

C

Modal / dialog window

A popup dialog with a mini video preview, trim handles, and export options. Focused, self-contained — you set your trim points and click Save. Closes when done.
